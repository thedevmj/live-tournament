Rules

do / don't · AI-slop guardrails

Do
  • — Square corners, 1px hairline borders
  • — One acid primary per view
  • — .u-label for every piece of meta text
  • — .mono + tabular-nums for changing numbers
  • — clamp() type for fluid headings/clocks
  • — Color always paired with a text label
Don't
  • — rounded-* or shadow-* classes
  • — a second accent hue (no blue, no purple)
  • — gradient fills beyond the body's static wash
  • — glassmorphism / blur cards
  • — scroll-triggered animation
  • — .display for sentences, color-only status
Slop check Current codebase passes: no purple-blue defaults, no gratuitous rounded cards, no glass panels, no scroll-jank, personality-rich type. Single finding: ink-mute at 4.3:1 on panel → bump to #7c848e.
